BACKGROUND.

Founded and registered under the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) in September of 2024, MV VAL CONSTRUCTION OPC is a wholly Filipino owned company which aims to deliver high quality services and satisfaction to our clients.

Founder Michael Vincent B. Valite — a Registered Civil Engineer since 2008 — brings extensive knowledge, hands-on experience, skills and technical know how on the field of construction.
